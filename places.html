Beautiful Places in Vietnam

Each destination has a different atmosphere, from peaceful mountain villages to lively coastal cities and historic streets.

QUANG NINH

Ha Long Bay

Famous for emerald water and dramatic limestone islands rising from the sea.

QUANG NAM

Hoi An

A charming historic town known for colorful streets, lanterns, and old architecture.

CENTRAL VIETNAM

Da Nang

A modern coastal city with beautiful beaches, mountains, and a relaxed atmosphere.

LAO CAI

Sa Pa

A mountain destination surrounded by valleys, rice terraces, and spectacular views.

KIEN GIANG

Phu Quoc

A tropical island destination with clear water, beaches, and beautiful sunsets.

TRAVEL TIP

Choose a destination that matches your mood.

Want nature? Try Sa Pa or Ha Long Bay.

Love history? Spend time exploring Hoi An.

Prefer the beach? Visit Da Nang or Phu Quoc.
